# Foundation models and segmentation backbones

| File | Paper | Why it matters for RouteSAM |
|---|---|---|
| `00_UNet_Ronneberger_2015.pdf` | U-Net | Canonical specialist network and direct single-image inference baseline. |
| `01_SAM_Kirillov_2023.pdf` | Segment Anything | Establishes promptable segmentation and the original SAM paradigm. |
| `02_SAM2_Ravi_2024.pdf` | SAM 2 | Introduces memory-based image/video propagation, the immediate precursor to pseudo-video transfer. |
| `03_SAM3_Carion_2025.pdf` | SAM 3 | RouteSAM's generalist foundation model; supports concept, exemplar and video-style propagation. |
| `04_MedSAM_Ma_2024.pdf` | MedSAM | Demonstrates medical-domain adaptation of SAM and motivates the domain-gap discussion. |
| `05_SAM_Medical_Experimental_Study_Mazurowski_2023.pdf` | SAM for Medical Image Analysis | Broad empirical evidence that zero-shot SAM performance varies strongly by medical task and prompt. |
| `06_SAM2_Medical_Benchmark_Ma_2024.pdf` | SAM in Medical Images and Videos | Medical benchmark for SAM2 and a useful source for discussing video propagation in medical data. |
